Privacy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we collect your name, email address, the type of business you run, whether you'd like updates from us, and which link brought you to this page.

Why we collect it

We use these details to contact you about Corvea's launch and founding-member offer, and to understand which kinds of businesses are interested. If you ticked the updates box, we'll also send you occasional news about Corvea. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your details. They're stored with our database provider (Supabase) and emails are sent through our email provider. Both only process your details on our behalf.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing hello@corvea.com.au. If you have a privacy concern, contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
